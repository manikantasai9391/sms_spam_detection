📩 SMS Spam Detection

🚨 What is Spam?

Spam messages are unwanted, fraudulent or promotional SMS that are sent to trick users into clicking links, sharing personal information or claiming fake rewards.

Common Examples

  • Congratulations! You won ₹50,000.
  • Claim your FREE gift now.
  • Click here to verify your bank account.
  • You have won a lottery prize.

✅ What is Ham?

Ham messages are genuine and safe messages received from friends, family, schools, offices and trusted organizations.

Common Examples

  • Hi, are you coming today?
  • Meeting starts at 3 PM.
  • Happy Birthday!
  • Please submit the assignment tomorrow.
